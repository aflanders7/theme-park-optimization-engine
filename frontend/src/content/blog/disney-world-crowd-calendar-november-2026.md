November is a lovely time to visit Walt Disney World. The weather is generally a little chilly, the holiday season is beginning, and you can experience the parks as they transition from fall to Christmas.

The biggest thing to plan around is Thanksgiving. Crowds tend to increase as the holiday approaches, and Thanksgiving week is one of the busiest travel periods of the year. However, **early November is a good time to visit Disney World overall.** The first half of the month can be a good option, but crowds can be very unpredictable during Thanksgiving week.

You can view the full [Disney World Crowd Calendar](/crowd-calendar) to compare predicted crowds for your dates.

## Is November a Good Time to Visit Disney World?

Yes! November can be a great time to visit, especially if you plan your trip before Thanksgiving week or want to experience Disney's holiday decorations.

A few things I love about November:

- **More comfortable weather:** November is often a welcome break from Florida's summer heat and humidity, although warm days and rain are still possible.
- **Holiday decorations:** Christmas decorations begin appearing in early November, with more decorations being added as the month progresses.
- **Seasonal entertainment:** Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party and Disney Jollywood Nights bring holiday entertainment to Magic Kingdom and Hollywood Studios on select nights.
- **EPCOT's holiday festival:** The EPCOT International Festival of the Holidays begins November 27, with seasonal food, entertainment, and holiday traditions from around the world.

The main drawback is Thanksgiving week. If your dates are flexible, the first half of November is generally worth considering before the holiday travel rush begins.

## The Best Days to Visit Disney World in November 2026

The best date depends on which park you want to visit. A day that looks good for Magic Kingdom may not be the best choice for EPCOT, so it's worth comparing parks individually rather than choosing one date for your entire trip.

### Best days for Magic Kingdom

Based on the MouseDays predictions, **November 1, 8, and 15** are the lowest-crowd options for Magic Kingdom, each with a predicted crowd level of 1.

November 6, 7, 10, 13, 20, and 29 also have relatively low predicted crowds, at level 2.

Magic Kingdom is expected to be very busy on Thanksigivng day. Choose a different park that day if possible.

If Magic Kingdom is your top priority, consider visiting earlier in the month. Just keep in mind that Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party takes place on select nights, and the park's regular operating hours can differ on party days.

### Best days for EPCOT

EPCOT's predictions look different from Magic Kingdom's. The lowest predicted crowd levels are on **November 1, 4, and 29**, each at level 5.

EPCOT is expected to be very busy on Thanksgiving day, but visitors reported that EPCOT was less busy than Magic Kingdom on Thanksgiving day 2025. If you are visiting during Thanksgiving week, EPCOT could be a good choice for Thanksgiving day.

### Best days for Hollywood Studios

Hollywood Studios has several lower predicted crowd days early in the month. November 1 is the lowest at level 1, while November 4, 7, 8, 13, 14, 15, and 28 are predicted at level 2.

Keep Disney Jollywood Nights in mind when choosing your dates. The event takes place on select evenings, and Hollywood Studios may have different operating hours on those nights.

### Best days for Animal Kingdom

Animal Kingdom's lowest predicted crowd level is 3, with November 1, 3, 4, 5, 10, 17, and 18 all falling into that category.

The park is predicted to be busiest from November 21–25, with crowd levels reaching 6.

## Thanksgiving Week at Disney World

Thanksgiving falls on **Thursday, November 26, 2026**. The MouseDays calendar flags November 21–29 as the main Thanksgiving crowd period, so plan for increased demand across the resort.

Thanksgiving week is not necessarily equally busy at every park. In reports from last year's holiday period, Magic Kingdom was described as very crowded, while EPCOT seemed only moderately crowded. Individual experiences vary, and past reports don't guarantee the same pattern in 2026. 

The MouseDays predictions also show differences between the parks. On Thanksgiving Day, the predicted crowd levels are:

| Park | November 26 prediction |
|---|---:|
| Magic Kingdom | 6 |
| EPCOT | 8 |
| Hollywood Studios | 4 |
| Animal Kingdom | 5 |

These numbers are a reminder to compare parks instead of assuming that every park will have the same crowd level. They are forecasts, not a promise that one park will feel quieter than another. If you are going during Thanksgiving week, be prepared for the parks to be busy.

### Tips for visiting during Thanksgiving week

- **Arrive early.** Rope dropping popular attractions can help you get more done before waits build.
- **Make dining reservations ahead of time.** Popular table-service restaurants can be difficult to book close to your visit.
- **Be flexible with park days.** Compare predicted crowds and consider which attractions matter most to your group.
- **Expect busy transportation and walkways.** Allow extra time to get between your hotel, transportation, and the parks.
- **Don't try to do everything.** Choose your must-do attractions and leave room for breaks, meals, and seasonal entertainment.

If Thanksgiving week is the only time you can travel, you can still have a wonderful trip. Just set realistic expectations and plan ahead.

## When Do Christmas Decorations Go Up at Disney World?

If you want to see Disney World decorated for Christmas, your travel dates matter.

The holiday transformation starts in early November. Magic Kingdom typically switches from Halloween to Christmas first, with decorations going up quickly after the final Halloween festivities. The other parks and resorts follow over the first part of the month.

**Disney's official 2026 holiday season begins November 13,** but decorations can appear before then. If seeing the Christmas decorations is important to you, mid-November is a safer target than the first week of the month.

EPCOT follows a slightly different schedule because its International Festival of the Holidays begins November 27. That is when you can enjoy the festival's seasonal food, entertainment, and holiday traditions.

Here's how I'd think about the timing:

| When you're visiting | What to expect |
|---|---|
| November 1–7 | The holiday transformation is underway, especially at Magic Kingdom, but decorations may not be complete throughout the resort. Lower crowds. |
| November 8–12 | More holiday decorations and seasonal entertainment should be up, especially at Magic Kingdom and Hollywood Studios. |
| November 13–20 | Most of the decorations will be up across Disney World. Crowds will fluctuate. |
| November 21–26 | The holidays are here, but Thanksgiving travel brings higher crowds. |
| November 27–30 | EPCOT's International Festival of the Holidays begins, making this a good time to visit if you don't mind the holiday crowds. |

Disney does not guarantee an exact completion date for every decoration at every park and resort, so treat these as general planning guidelines rather than a day-by-day installation schedule.

## Disney World Events in November 2026

November brings the start of Disney's holiday events, including two separately ticketed nighttime parties and EPCOT's holiday festival.

| Date | Event | Location |
|---|---|---|
| November 7, 14, 16, 21, 23, 28 | Disney Jollywood Nights | Hollywood Studios |
| November 8, 9, 12, 13, 15, 17, 19, 20, 24, 25, 27, 29 | Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party | Magic Kingdom |
| November 11 | Veterans Day | All parks |
| November 21–29 | Thanksgiving crowd period | All parks |
| November 26 | Thanksgiving Day | All parks |
| November 27–30 | EPCOT International Festival of the Holidays | EPCOT |

Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party and Disney Jollywood Nights require separate event tickets. On party nights, Hollywood Studios and Magic Kingdom close earlier than on a typical night, so check the operating hours for your specific date before making plans.

The EPCOT International Festival of the Holidays begins on November 27 and runs through December 30, 2026. It's a great option if you enjoy seasonal food and holiday storytelling.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is November a crowded month at Disney World?

It depends on when you visit. The first half of November can be a good time to go, while Thanksgiving week is generally a busier travel period. Crowd levels also vary by park and date.

### Is Disney World busy on Thanksgiving Day?

Thanksgiving Day can be very busy, but crowds don't necessarily look the same at every park. Check the predictions for your specific date and make a plan for your must-do attractions.

### When should I visit Disney World to see Christmas decorations?

Mid-November is a good target if you want to see most of Disney World decorated for the holidays. Magic Kingdom begins its transformation earlier, while EPCOT's International Festival of the Holidays starts November 27 in 2026.

### Is EPCOT less crowded than Magic Kingdom during Thanksgiving week?

Not necessarily. Some visitors reported that EPCOT felt more manageable than Magic Kingdom during last year's Thanksgiving period, but that isn't guaranteed again this year. 

### Should I avoid Disney World during Thanksgiving week?

Not if it's the best time for your family to travel! Expect high wait times, plan ahead, and prioritize the experiences you care about most. If you have flexibility and want to avoid the Thanksgiving rush, consider an earlier November trip.

## Final Thoughts

November can be a wonderful time to visit Walt Disney World, especially if you want to experience the beginning of the holiday season. The first half of the month will likely have moderate crowds, but crowds will grow towards the end of the month. Thanksgiving week will require more planning and realistic expectations.
