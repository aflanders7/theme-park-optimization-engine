December is one of the most festive times to visit Walt Disney World, but it can also be one of the busiest. Crowds tend to increase as Christmas approaches, making the timing of your trip especially important.

MouseDays uses historical Disney World crowd patterns and statistical forecasting to estimate how busy each park is likely to be on each day. Here's what to know about visiting Disney World in December 2026.

## The Best Days to Visit Disney World in December 2026

If you're looking for lower-crowd days, **the beginning of December is generally your best bet**. Crowds typically build throughout the month as Christmas and New Year's approach.

Sunday, December 6 is currently expected to have the lowest crowds of the month. For a quieter trip, consider visiting between December 1–10. Crowd levels will start increasing around December 14.

December 21–31 are expected to have very high crowd levels. If you're visiting during this period, consider purchasing Lightning Lane in advance to help reduce your wait times for the most popular attractions.

## December 2026 Disney World Events

December at Walt Disney World is packed full of fun and festive holiday events.

### Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party

**Magic Kingdom:** December 1, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11, 13, 15, 17, 18, 20, 22

Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party takes place from 7:00 PM to midnight and requires a separate ticket. On party days, Magic Kingdom closes to regular guests earlier than usual, but **crowds are typically lower earlier in the day**. Keep the party schedule in mind when planning your Magic Kingdom days.

### Disney Jollywood Nights

**Hollywood Studios:** December 5, 7, 12, 14, 19, 21, 23

Jollywood Nights is a separately ticketed after-hours event running from 7:30 PM to 12:30 AM on select nights.

### EPCOT International Festival of the Holidays

**EPCOT:** Daily through December 30

This holiday festival is included with regular EPCOT admission and features seasonal food, entertainment, and celebrations throughout the park. Because of this, EPCOT is consistently the busiest park throughout December. Use the [Park Planner](/parks) to determine the best day to visit EPCOT during your trip.

### Candlelight Processional

**EPCOT:** Daily through December 30

The Candlelight Processional features Christmas carols and a telling of the Christmas story at EPCOT's America Gardens Theatre. The show is included with regular EPCOT admission, and dining packages are available for guests who want reserved seating.

### Christmas Eve & Christmas Day

**December 24–25**

These are among the busiest days of the year at Walt Disney World. If you're visiting during Christmas week, expect significantly higher crowd levels and plan your park days carefully.

### New Year's Eve

**December 31**

New Year's Eve brings major celebrations across Walt Disney World and typically some of the highest crowds of the year. Consider visiting Magic Kingdom instead of EPCOT for lower crowds.
