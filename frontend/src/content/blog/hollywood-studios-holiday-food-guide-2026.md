The holidays are arriving at Disney's Hollywood Studios! From festive cupcakes and gingerbread cookies to seasonal treats inspired by Star Wars, there are several holiday snacks to look forward to this year.

Here's where to find all the daytime holiday food at Hollywood Studios in 2026.

## Hollywood Studios Holiday Food 2026

The holiday food offerings at Hollywood Studios begin November 7 and typically are available through January 5, 2027.
