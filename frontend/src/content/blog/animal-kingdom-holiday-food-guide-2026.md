The holidays are coming to Disney's Animal Kingdom, and that means festive desserts, seasonal snacks, and plenty of Christmas treats! Disney has released its 2026 holiday food guide, featuring new treats and returning favorites throughout the park.

## Animal Kingdom Holiday Food 2026

Most of this year's holiday offerings become available starting November 6 and will be around through January 3, 2027. Santa's Holiday Grove opens November 13, so some of its special holiday treats won't be available right away.

Here's where to find this year's holiday food throughout Animal Kingdom!

## Discovery Island

### Terra Treats

Available November 6 – January 3.

- **Festive Cookie Dough Brownie Ice Cream Sandwich (New!)**

- **Reindeer Sundae (New!)**

Available November 6 – January 3.

- **Gingerbread Cheesecake Pop (New!)**

![A gingerbread cheesecake pop covered in chocolate](/blog/Gingerbread-Cheesecake-Pop.WebP)

### Creature Comforts

Available November 6 – January 3.

- **Snowflake Cinnamon Roll (New!)**

### Isle of Java

Available November 6 – January 3.

## Santa's Holiday Grove

Santa's Holiday Grove is a special holiday food location near Theater in the Wild. It opens November 13 and features several new seasonal treats, festive drinks, and savory snacks.

Available November 13 – January 3.

- **Peppermint-Chocolate Cookie Dough Brownie Ice Cream Sandwich (New!)**
- **Pistachio Cookie (New!)**
- **Pan de Jamon (New!)**
- **Chocolate Tree Croissant (New!)**
- **Santa Hat Croissant (New!)**
- **Jalapeño-Cheddar Pretzel (New!)**
- **Cookie Butter Milkshake (New!)**
![Cookie butter milkshake topped with whipped cream, caramel, and a chocolate-dipped cookie](/blog/Cookie-Butter-Milkshake.WebP)
- **Chilled Mulled Wine (New!)**
- **Festive Chocolate Chip Cookie (New!)**
- **Mixed Berry Focaccia (New!)**
- **Chocolate-Dipped Mickey Gingerbread**

## Festive Sweets Across Walt Disney World

In addition to the park's regular holiday food offerings, Disney has several seasonal treats available at multiple locations throughout Walt Disney World. If you're visiting Animal Kingdom, stop by Zuri's Sweets Shop to find these festive favorites!

### Zuri's Sweets Shop

- **Minnie Reindeer Caramel Apple (New!)**
- **Santa Mickey Caramel Apple (New!)**
- **Mickey Santa Hat Cereal Treat**
- **Mickey Snowman Cereal Treat**
- **Large Mickey Gingerbread Cookie**
- **Large Mickey Holiday Sugar Cookie**
- **Mickey Peppermint Chocolate Cookie Sandwich**
- **Mickey Holiday Chocolate Cupcake (New!)**
- **Minnie Holiday Vanilla Cupcake (New!)**

These treats are also available at select locations throughout Walt Disney World, including Magic Kingdom, EPCOT, Disney Springs, and select resort hotels. Availability varies by location.
