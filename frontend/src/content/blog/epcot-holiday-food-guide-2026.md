The EPCOT International Festival of the Holidays is returning for 2026, bringing festive food, seasonal drinks, and holiday traditions from around the world! With more than 20 Holiday Kitchens throughout EPCOT, there are plenty of new treats and returning favorites to try this year.

The festival runs from **November 27 through December 30, 2026**. Whether you're looking for holiday cookies, savory dishes, or festive drinks, here's where to find the 2026 Festival of the Holidays food at EPCOT.

## EPCOT Festival of the Holidays Food 2026

This year's festival features 186 food and beverage offerings, including 98 new items across 20 Holiday Kitchens. There are also two new Holiday Kitchens: Diwali Holiday Kitchen and Season's Eatings.

I've organized the offerings by location so you can find each treat as you explore EPCOT.

## Holiday Cookie Stroll

The Holiday Cookie Stroll is returning for 2026! Guests can purchase select cookies from participating Holiday Kitchens and EPCOT locations, collect stamps in their Festival Passport, and redeem a completed passport for a special treat at Holiday Sweets & Treats.

To complete the stroll, purchase five eligible cookies and collect five stamps. Look for the cookie symbol in the festival menus to identify participating treats.

Here are the 2026 Holiday Cookie Stroll offerings:

- **Ginger-Molasses Cookie** – Experimental Prototype Cookies of Tomorrow
- **Cranberry-Oatmeal Cookie** – Connections Café
- **Christmas Sugar Cookie** – Holiday Hearth Desserts
- **Hazelnut Linzer Cookie** – Bavaria Holiday Kitchen
- **Chocolate Cookie** – American Holiday Table
- **Raspberry-Walnut Rugelach** – L'Chaim! Holiday Kitchen
- **Mickey-shaped Almond Black and White Cookie** – L'Chaim! Holiday Kitchen
- **Maple Leaf Shortbread Cookie** – Yukon Holiday Kitchen
- **Coffee-Mocha Cookie** – Mele Kalikimaka Holiday Kitchen

## Planning Your Festival Visit

The EPCOT International Festival of the Holidays runs from November 27 through December 30, 2026. Holiday Kitchens are spread throughout World Showcase and the other areas of the park, so give yourself plenty of time to explore the menus.

The My Disney Experience app can help you check current food availability and operating hours during your visit. Remember that festival offerings are subject to change and availability.

## More Holiday Planning at Disney World

EPCOT is a great place to experience holiday traditions from around the world, especially during the International Festival of the Holidays. Along with the food, you can enjoy the Holiday Storytellers, Voices of Liberty Dickens Carolers, JOYFUL! A Celebration of the Season, and the Candlelight Processional.

If you're planning a November or December visit, check out the [MouseDays Crowd Calendar](/crowd-calendar) to compare predicted crowd levels and choose your park days.

You can also use the [Disney World Park Planner](/parks) to organize your visit.
