The holidays are coming to Magic Kingdom, and that means seasonal snacks, festive desserts, and holiday drinks are returning to the park! Disney has released its 2026 holiday food guide, featuring new treats and returning favorites throughout the Christmas season.

## Magic Kingdom Holiday Food 2026

Most of this year's holiday offerings become available starting November 8 and will be around through January 2, 2027. Some items have different availability dates, so check the details below before making a special trip for a particular treat.
